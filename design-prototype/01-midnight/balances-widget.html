Balances widget

The fourth home-screen widget (SPEC-UI-UX §6.25, UI-109). It shows the total of your bank, wallet and cash balances and up to three accounts, largest first. Credit cards are not in the total. Tapping anywhere opens My accounts. Same black tile, type sizes and Hide amounts rule as the three widgets you already have. Example figures below.
